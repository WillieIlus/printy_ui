- Repository revision: current repository state retrieved from the live GitHub branch at the time of review.

SECTION 12. ROLE AND DATA MATRIX
=================================

12.1 Matrix summary
- Anonymous visitor: sees public calculator, pricing range or sample, no full imposition
- Buyer: sees total price, job summary, due date, stage status
- Manager: sees buyer data plus cost details, pricing layers, press sheet, layout, profit margin, fee tier, printer payout
- Printer: sees job summary, due date, sheet math, payout, no buyer total or cost breakdown beyond payout
- Admin: sees operational oversight and verification states

12.2 Important privacy and visibility rules observed
- buyer does not see imposition or sheet counts
- manager sees more operational detail than buyer
- printer sees own payout and production detail but not buyer total
- public visitor sees gated quote summary but not internal pricing detail

SECTION 13. INTERACTION AND RESPONSIVE RULES
============================================

13.1 Lists and grouping
- Public pages and dashboard lists group items by server-side status or section.
- Quote and job cards open in place, under their own header, with visible open state.
- Completed or muted cards remain visually subdued and are last in group order.

13.2 Job code chip
- Every job and quote card shows a code chip in the footer and opens a track page when the chip is tapped.
- Copy/share behaviour is part of the product; it preserves tracking and quote visibility rules.

13.3 Refresh and countdown behaviour
- The app includes stage tracking and time remaining; public buyer tracking and job tracking are time-aware and update as the job advances.
- The design uses consistent stage badges and time status, with overdue states highlighted.

13.4 Mobile behaviour
- Public pages stack sections one column on mobile.
- Dashboard nav collapses into compact layouts; main content remains single-column; cards and side rails reflow to vertical flow.
- Buttons remain thumb-friendly and stacked as needed.

13.5 Accessibility
- Focus states are green and visible; inputs are styled with outlined borders and clear focus rings.
- There is a light and dark app feel depending on surface; contrast is generally strong on light paper backgrounds.
- Screen-reader detail and hidden labels were not fully audited at the level of each page in this repo pass.
